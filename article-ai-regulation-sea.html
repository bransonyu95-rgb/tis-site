What This Means for Manufacturers

If you supply multinational customers, compliance teams will soon check your AI practices too. Regulations increasingly require documentation: what AI tools you use, what data they process, and how humans oversee decisions. European customers already comply with the EU AI Act, and their supply chain audits now ask about AI usage and data governance. If you can't answer those questions, you become a supply chain risk.

The Compliance Advantage

Move first and regulation becomes a competitive advantage, not a cost. Manufacturers who set up AI governance early become preferred suppliers for customers that need compliant supply chains. The factory that documents its AI policies wins the audit. The one that can't loses the contract.

Don't wait for the regulation to arrive. Prepare your AI documentation now, before your first customer audit asks for it.
