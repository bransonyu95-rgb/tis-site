# The Industrial Scribe

> Frameworks and AI prompts for office staff in Southeast Asian manufacturing: planners, QC executives, purchasing and business-development coordinators. Written by Ah Hock, a pen name, from inside a real Malaysian manufacturing office. For office staff, never for programmers.

Ah Hock publishes only what he has run on real work, uses real numbers, and never names a customer. There is no single standard method in manufacturing: each piece starts by diagnosing the nature of the business, then customises the workflow.
