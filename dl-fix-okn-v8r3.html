Way A · any AI chat, nothing to install

  1. Unzip the folder and open paste-prompt.md.
  2. Choose proposal, deck, dashboard, check or rehearse. Fill the blanks: who decides, what you need them to say yes to.
  3. Paste your table and send. Build the slides in your own PowerPoint or Excel from what it gives you.

Way B · the Claude Code skill

  1. Needs Claude Pro (USD 20/month) with Claude Code installed.
  2. Move the get-it-approved folder into your skills folder: ~/.claude/skills/ on Mac, %USERPROFILE%\.claude\skills\ on Windows. Cannot see the hidden folder? Ask Claude Code to move it for you.
  3. Drop your Excel or CSV export in and say help me get this approved or build my dashboard. It does the maths with a script and builds the file.

The references/ folder is the playbook itself, seven files worth reading on their own. The examples/ folder has a worked deck, a worked dashboard, the dashboard file the skill built, and a headcount proposal, all from invented data labelled EXAMPLE.

Honest limits

It uses only your numbers and will not invent a saving, a target, an owner or an opinion. It cannot make a bad idea good; the decision is still the decider's. Follow your company's rules on putting work data into AI tools; placeholders work just as well.
