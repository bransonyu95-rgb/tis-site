Refunds.

No refund guarantee. Here is how to not need one.

The position: everything sold here is a digital download or a web module you keep. It is delivered the moment you pay and cannot be handed back, so there is no refund guarantee.

Look before you buy

  • The 5-minute preview. The kit page has a click-through of the whole first sitting, every screen, before any payment: open the preview.
  • The honest requirements. You need Claude Pro (USD 20/month, paid to Anthropic) with Claude Code installed. If you do not have that or do not want it, the kit is not for you yet.
  • The real cases. The numbers on the homepage are our own. Nobody promises you the same.
  • The free things. The Insider Test, the free checklist and the free skill cost nothing and show how this place thinks.

If something goes wrong after you buy

  • It will not run. Reply to your purchase receipt. A human helps you get it running, usually within a day. One honest limit: it cannot fix a business problem that is not a drafting problem.
  • The link never arrived, or you were charged twice by mistake. Email theindustrialscribe@gmail.com from the address you paid with. That is a mistake to put right, not a refund question.
  • You bought the wrong bundle. Say so in the same email. The seller will look at it case by case.

Updates are free

Whatever you bought, every update to that product lands on your download page at no charge. Buying early never costs you later.
