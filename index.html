How it works

Three steps. No code. No learning curve.

These are tools for people doing the work — not tools for people who want to be software engineers.

01

Pick a skill.

Each skill is a structured prompt — written by an operator — that encodes the logic, format, and judgement of one specific manufacturing workflow.

02

Copy & paste.

One click copies the full skill. Paste it as the first message into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or any LLM you already use. No account here, no install.

03

Drop your data.

Paste your raw job list, defect note, or process description after the skill. Your LLM produces the artifact — formatted, clean, and shop-floor-ready.

A skill is a structured prompt — usually 300-800 words — that you paste into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or any LLM as your first message. It encodes a specific manufacturing workflow: the role, the inputs, the output format, the domain rules, the edge cases. After you paste it, your LLM behaves like a 15-year shop-floor operator for that one task.
