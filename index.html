How it works

Three steps. No code. No learning curve.

These are tools for people doing the work — not tools for people who want to be software engineers.

01

Drop your email.

We send the templates and playbooks straight to your inbox. No account to create, no dashboard to log into.

02

Open the template.

Excel, Word, or PDF — familiar tools. Read the one-page playbook that comes with each one. Swap in your data.

03

Send the report.

Hand it off to your boss, your auditor, your client. Go home on time. Repeat next week in half the time.
