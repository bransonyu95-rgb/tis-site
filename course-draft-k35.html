See the Waste in a Factory Office

A course by Ah Hock, written from inside a real Malaysian manufacturing office

  • Beginner
  • 14 short lessons
  • 54 minutes in all
  • 1 thing you make
InformationLessonsProject

Every factory office has work that nobody needs. A report that nobody reads. A form that four people sign. You can feel it, and soon you will be able to point at it.

In this course Ah Hock shows you one small step in each short lesson. You learn to see the waste, then to count it in hours. At the end you hold one page that you made: your own waste map.

Who it is for

Fresh graduates and people changing career who want a factory office job. No factory job needed to start: you practise on a made-up factory.
