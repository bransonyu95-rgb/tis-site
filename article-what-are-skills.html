Stop Re-Introducing Yourself to Your AI Every Morning

The Industrial Scribe | AI Fundamentals  ·  2 min read  ·  March 2026

Every new AI chat, the same ritual. "I'm a BD manager at a CNC factory." "These are our customer codes." "Exclude the Preclosure rows." Ten minutes of introductions before any work starts — and when the chat ends, the AI forgets you ever met.

There's a fix, and it's the same fix factories invented for human beings decades ago: write the SOP once, and stop briefing every new operator from scratch.

A Skill is an SOP for your AI

A Skill is one instruction file — usually called SKILL.md — that teaches the AI one job the way you do it: your workflow, your terms, your quality rules. Install it once. From then on, the AI reads it automatically whenever that job comes up, in every conversation, without being asked.

ComponentWhat It DoesExample
Trigger descriptionTells the AI when to use this Skill"Use when the user uploads WIP/ERP data"
Step-by-step workflowThe process to follow"1. Profile data 2. Flag anomalies 3. Ask human to confirm"
Domain knowledgeFacts the AI wouldn't know"Preclosure rows should be flagged for exclusion"
Quality rulesStandards to maintain"Never auto-delete data without human confirmation"
Output formatHow results should look"Always provide both a table AND a chart"
LimitationsWhat the AI should NOT do"Revenue forecasts must come from the human"

Two from my own toolbox

The Factory Data Analyst

Teaches the AI to handle WIP data my way: profile the data first, flag Preclosure rows, calculate NPI rates, cross-reference revenue across currencies. Without the Skill, that's a ten-minute briefing every session. With it, I paste the export and go make kopi.

The Negotiation Advisor

Turns the AI into a sparring partner that opens with the customer's objections, stress-tests every draft, and strips the internal language out before anything leaves the building.

Installing one is nothing: download the file, drop it into your AI tool's skills folder, done. No coding, no restart.

ApproachHow It WorksLimitation
Copy-paste promptYou paste instructions at the start of every chatYou forget steps. It's tedious. Inconsistent.
Saved templateYou keep a text file and paste from itBetter, but still manual. Doesn't auto-trigger.
SkillAI reads it automatically when relevantPermanent. Consistent. Zero effort after install.

The point

Your best people's judgment, written down once, applied every time. That's all a Skill is. The factory that documents its know-how beats the factory that keeps it in heads — and the same rule now applies to the AI on your desk.
