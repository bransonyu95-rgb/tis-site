Questions.

What do I need?

Claude Pro (USD 20/month, from claude.com) with Claude Code installed, a Windows or Mac computer, and 20 to 30 minutes for the first sitting. If Claude Code is not installed yet, add 10 to 15 minutes once.

Is it really one payment?

For the kit, yes: USD 3.99 once, every update free. Claude Pro is separate and you pay Anthropic for that, whether or not you buy the kit. There are no credits and nothing to subscribe to here.

Will it save me hours?

Nobody honest can promise that. What it does: in the first sitting it drafts your real document from your real file. You time the before and after yourself. The real cases on this page are the only numbers quoted, and they are our own.

Do I need to be technical?

No. It was built by a non-technical factory office worker, for non-technical office people. If you can unzip a folder and answer five plain questions, you are qualified.

Is my company data safe?

Everything runs on your own computer through your own Claude account. Your files go to Anthropic the same way anything you type into Claude does, and nowhere else. Nothing reaches The Industrial Scribe. Follow your company's rules on putting work information into AI tools; placeholders like "Customer A" work just as well.

What if it doesn't work for me?

There is no refund guarantee: the kit is a download you keep. So look before you buy: the 5-minute preview on the kit page shows the first sitting click by click. If something breaks after you buy, reply to your receipt and a human answers, usually within a day.

Is this made by Anthropic?

No. The Industrial Scribe is an independent one-person publication. It works with Claude and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic.
