The Waste Hunt.
See it, cut it, then automate what's left.

You know your weekly report, your PO keying or your supplier chasing eats hours. You have heard "use AI". But which part, and how? Most people automate the task exactly as it is, waste included, and get a faster mess. The Hunt finds the waste first.

Get it USD 3.99 once About 30 minutes with one real task in front of you.

What it does, in order

  1. Your factory first. Seven questions: what you make, volume pattern, typical customer, how orders arrive, how many could do your job, what a new customer needs, and how much of your sales sits with your biggest one or two customers. That gives your business nature, which says where waste costs your kind of factory most.
  2. One real task, step by step. It asks for your written SOP, or notes that there is none, then keeps asking until it knows what really happens: who, which system, what is copied from where to where, how many minutes, how often, who waits, what gets redone. It digs for the hidden steps: searching, chasing, retyping, reformatting, waiting for a signature.
  3. The waste map. Every step tagged with the office version of the eight wastes, with hours per month from your own numbers.
  4. Cut before you automate. Eliminate, combine, rearrange or simplify first. Some hunts save hours with no tool at all.
  5. What to automate, and how far. Each remaining step gets a level, from "fix the template" to "an AI skill that runs on your files". The top three are ranked by hours, ease and risk.
  6. A build guide for number one. What to prepare, the exact prompt or build steps, a test on one real case, before-and-after timing, and the updated SOP.

The hard rule: anything that leaves or commits the company (sending, approving, ordering, pricing, signing) stays a human click. AI drafts, a person decides. Anything that connects to company systems needs IT's OK first.

Two ways to run it

A · No install, any AI chat

Open paste-prompt.md, copy the box into a new chat in ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, free plans included, and answer one question at a time. About 30 minutes.

B · Claude Code skill

For people who already use Claude Code (needs Claude Pro, USD 20/month). Move the folder to your skills folder, drop your SOP, a sample report or an export into sop-in/, and say waste hunt. It reads your files, does the sums with a script, saves every hunt, and keeps a log of hours found and hours actually saved. If you want, it then builds number one with you and tests it on your real file.

What's inside the download

  • paste-prompt.md: the no-install version.
  • SKILL.md: the Claude Code skill.
  • tis-resources.md: the free and paid TIS pages it may point you to, only when one fits a finding in your waste map.
  • examples/: a worked hunt on an invented company, labelled EXAMPLE.

The honest limits

  • It uses only your numbers. Hours saved are an estimate from your answers until you time the before and after.
  • It finds waste in office workflows: reports, data entry, chasing, approvals, documents. It is not a shop-floor time study.
  • Follow your company's rules on putting work information into AI tools. Placeholders like "Customer A" work just as well.
