Privacy.

Short, because there is not much to tell.

What this site collects

  • Your email, if you give it. The Insider Letters sign-up and the Insider Test email box go to MailerLite, the newsletter service this site uses. Every email has an unsubscribe link. Unsubscribing removes you.
  • What you bought. Payments run through Stripe. Stripe handles your card; this site never sees your card number. The seller sees your email address and the product you bought, which is how your download link and support work.
  • Nothing else. There is no analytics script, no advertising pixel and no tracking cookie on this site.

What stays in your browser

The Insider Test, the web modules' checklists and a few small conveniences remember your answers inside your own browser only. That data never leaves your device and is not sent to this site. Clearing your browser storage clears it.

The MailerLite form script may set its own cookie so a form you already filled does not show again. That is MailerLite's cookie, under MailerLite's privacy policy.

Where your files go when you use a kit

The kits run on your own computer through your own Claude account. When the kit reads your export or drafts your report, that content goes to Anthropic the same way anything you type into Claude does, under Anthropic's privacy policy. It does not go to The Industrial Scribe. Nobody here can see your files, your reports or your prompts.

Follow your company's rules on putting work information into AI tools. Placeholders like "Customer A" work just as well.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe from any email with the link at the bottom.
  • Want your email address deleted from the newsletter list and the sales records this site keeps? Write to theindustrialscribe@gmail.com from that address. Stripe's own records of a payment stay with Stripe under their policy.

Who to ask

This site is run by one person in Malaysia. Questions go to theindustrialscribe@gmail.com, answered by a human, usually within a day.
