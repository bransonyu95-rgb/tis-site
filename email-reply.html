Email Intelligence Desk.
Paste the customer email chain. Get the history, your rules checked, and a reply in your voice.

A customer chases you for the third time. The thread is 15 emails deep, with forwards, quoted replies and three people copied. You need to know what was promised, what is overdue and what they are actually asking, and you need to answer well, fast.

Get it USD 9.99 once Paste box for any AI chat, or a Claude Code skill. Drafts only; you press Send.

What you get, every time

  1. The catch-up. Who's who, what happened and when, every promise with its status (kept, open, overdue), what the customer is asking now, what is still open, and their mood. It also flags "agreed by silence": things you never answered that the customer may now treat as agreed.
  2. The rules check. Any conflict with your goals or a past decision ("this goes against your decision of 12 Oct: no free air freight for this customer"), and any mismatch inside the thread, in at most three lines. It never settles the conflict on its own. You choose.
  3. One recommendation. If there is a real choice, two or three options in one line each, and the one it recommends with the reason.
  4. The reply draft. In your language (English, Bahasa Melayu, Chinese and others), in your tone, with your sign-off. It answers their question in the first two sentences. Every promise has an owner and a date. Gaps are marked [CHECK].
  5. The fact check. Every date, number and promise in the draft is checked against the thread. A date nobody confirmed never goes out as a promise.
  6. A conversation, not a one-shot. Say "firmer", "shorter", "in Malay", or paste back your own edit: it changes only what you ask and tells you what changed. Ask "what do I owe?" and it lists every open promise across all your customers, oldest first.

It learns how you write, and who you write to

Your style, once, in five minutes. With your permission it reads a few emails you already sent: how you greet, sign off, say no, give bad news, chase. It shows you "this is how you write", asks if it got you right, then asks how you want to sound from now on. Only a short style summary is kept; no email text, names or prices.

A card per customer. The people and how each likes to be written to, what the customer is pushing for right now, their sensitivities, past cases, open promises. Before drafting it reads that card, so the reply fits the exact person, and it spots repeats. Cards grow as you work, and only you approve what is saved.

Your rules, optional. Your goals in order, how you decide, decisions already taken. One rule is always on: it never shows a customer your internal cost, margin, rates or supplier names.

Two ways to run it

A · No install, any AI chat

Open paste-prompt.md. Run the style builder once with 5 to 10 of your sent emails and keep the MY STYLE block it gives you. Each time, paste your copy plus the thread into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. Free plans work.

B · Claude Code skill

For people who already use Claude Code (needs Claude Pro, USD 20/month). Move the email-intelligence-desk folder to your skills folder, paste a thread and say "help me reply to this". On first use it asks permission and learns your style from emails you paste, drop into my-emails/, or from your Sent folder if your Claude already has an email connection. It never asks for your password.

The honest limits

  • It only knows what is in the thread, the customer's card and what you tell it. A promise made on the phone has to be told to it.
  • It drafts. It does not decide, and it never sends. Money, dates and admissions of fault are yours; it marks them [CHECK] rather than guess.
  • Check your company's rule on pasting customer emails into AI tools. If unsure, swap names, prices and part numbers for placeholders first.
  • Tested on 9 November 2026 in three role-play runs, not with paying customers yet. It kept internal cost and supplier names out of a mis-forwarded thread, flagged conflicts with logged decisions, and marked unconfirmed dates.
