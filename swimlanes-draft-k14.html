The two views
  1. Workflow. A swimlane read left to right. Each lane is who does the work: you, the model, scripts with no AI. Click any box: what goes in, what happens inside, what comes out, which rule files it follows.
  2. Trace. What actually ran, rebuilt read-only from Claude Code's own session logs on your computer: the real model used, every command and result, red for failures, and checks showing where reality drifted from the design.

The first trace almost always finds a surprise: a step that runs but is not on the map, a helper model that never gets used, a task silently switching models. That is the point.
